GD Tachometer is a tongue-in-cheek counter for involuntary invocations of a deity (G) or a dog (D) during particularly demanding workdays.

Because sometimes the best stress thermometer is counting how many times you said something you wouldn't say in front of children.

The tachometer tracks daily counts and automatically sets the next day's limit based on the previous day's total — so you can monitor the trajectory of your inner peace over time.
